3B3TPE // Rules & Policy

Terms

These terms describe the boundaries of the 3B3TPE server and community.

The server hosts the survival gamemode as provided by Mojang, an open ended sandbox, in which there are no inherent rules related to gameplay elements like PvP and griefing. This enables a chaotic and harsh world where players can fight and destroy whoever and whatever they wish if they choose.

However, it does not in any way invite or incite players to be toxic or harassing beyond the game.

Community Guidelines

3b3tpe encourages all players to remember the Minecraft Usage Guidelines, the Minecraft Community Standards, and the Xbox Community Standards that you agreed to when creating your Minecraft account.

Do not attempt to undermine the server's efforts to provide every player an equal and fair playing environment, through means such as abusing any exploit, bug or flawed game mechanic to cause server disruption or lag.

Anarchy

3b3tpe is an anarchy Minecraft server. This means that most in-game behavior is allowed, including:

  • cheating and hacked clients,
  • spamming,
  • griefing and raiding.

Strictly Prohibited

The following are strictly prohibited and may result in permanent removal from server and other consequences:

  • DDoS attacks or attempts to disrupt connectivity of the server, infrastructure or other players and
  • any illegal content involving minors or actions that put minors in danger.

Within these limits, we aim to keep in-game and community speech as free as reasonably possible.

Enforcement

_MasterZero may, at his discretion, remove or restrict access to users whose behavior makes the server unusable for others or clearly violates these rules.

_MasterZero and 3b3tpe reserve the right to suspend or terminate your access to the Minecraft server at any time, with or without notice, if:

  • you violate these Terms,
  • you engage in behaviour that is clearly harmful to the server or other users, or
  • we are required to do so by technical, security or legal reasons.
