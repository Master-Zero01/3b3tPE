3B3TPE // History

About
3B3TPE

A server built from a small survival community into a persistent crossplay anarchy world.

Goldcrest Anarchy

3b3tpe started out as a small server known as Goldcrest Anarchy on October 17th, 2021 as a regular survival server on version 1.17. Initially it was played by a small group of friends and promoted on places like Discord and Reddit to get some new players to join. It was made crossplay using GeyserMC and lasted nearly two years, its final day was May 25th, 2023.

The Beginning of 3B3TPE

The server owner, _MasterZero, took a break, moving on to playing other servers and other games. In the beginning of 2025, _MasterZero began to make plans to run another server, with the goal of creating a truly free and open world for Bedrock players to enjoy anarchy, free from abuse and broken server mechanics that the Bedrock anarchy community has been accustomed to. This was the beginning of 3b3tpe.

The New Map

The new map opened to the public on June 25th, 2025. A new community was formed and it was decided that as long as there is interest, the server would remain online. The community began as Bedrock-only players, who later decided they wanted complete crossplay, so GeyserMC was added. A few months later, _MasterZero decided to bring back the Goldcrest Anarchy map in a unique way: By combining the old map into the new one. You'll find remnants of the old map across the server, history yet to be told. This leads to the present day, a map that contains history as far as 5 years.

Present Day

3b3tpe is hosted and developed by _MasterZero. You can contact them on Discord, Reddit, or here on our forum page.
